# Design Direction

## Concept

**Impact in Motion** expresses an idea gathering energy. The experience moves from the energetic hero wave, through rigorous editorial structures, into a calm horizon-like finale.

## System

- Obsidian `#080A0B`, deep black `#030506`, warm ivory `#F1EFE7`, paper `#F7F5EF`, electric cobalt `#1945F5`.
- Barlow Condensed for monumental industrial display, Instrument Serif Italic for expressive contrast, Inter for reading, IBM Plex Mono for editorial metadata.
- Asymmetric twelve-column compositions on desktop; recomposed single-column editorial layouts on mobile.
- Thin rules, registration marks, section markers and a hand-drawn cobalt gesture connect the chapters.

## Art direction

The hero uses generated photoreal ocean imagery with screen compositing so the live HTML headline appears embedded in the scene. Other chapters change material language—paper, studio photography, engineered metal, orbital graphics and an abstract water horizon—rather than repeating literal water everywhere.

No supplied mockup image was present in the attachment directory, so the detailed written mockup specification was treated as authoritative.
