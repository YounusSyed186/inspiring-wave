## Verified business information

Services, testimonials, `info@inspiringwave.in`, `+91 7893801806`, Hyderabad location, and the March 2026 journal article were checked against the live website. Client names appear only where tied to published testimonials.

## Deliberate approximations

- The attachment contained only the text specification; no landing-page mockup image was available.
- No authentic project media was accessible in the empty repository. Portfolio entries are therefore clearly labeled capability/concept studies rather than fabricated client work.
- No existing enquiry backend or approved delivery credentials existed. The form prepares a real email and explicitly explains the handoff.
- Cinematic ambient movement is layered CSS/image compositing rather than video footage or a real-time fluid simulation. It is not represented as photoreal footage.

## Architecture

The DOM remains the readable source of all content. WebGL was intentionally omitted: the generated photography plus lightweight compositing achieved higher visual quality at far lower transfer, GPU and lifecycle cost.
